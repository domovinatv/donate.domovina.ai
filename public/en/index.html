What is DOMOVINA

DOMOVINA is an umbrella project spanning multiple open-source repositories under github.com/domovinatv.

Podcast corpus

Systematic transcription and indexing of Croatian podcasts with speaker recognition.

MCP servers

Public AI tools for searching the corpus via Claude.ai and other MCP clients.

mcp.domovina.ai →

Public services

Election tools, SMS services, maps, and other initiatives in the public interest.

Transparency

The entire project codebase is open and publicly available at github.com/domovinatv. Donations are primarily used for:

  • Cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare R2, Coolify VPS, ClickHouse, PostgreSQL)
  • AI APIs (Anthropic Claude, Google Vertex AI, embedding models)
  • Diarization and transcription (NVIDIA Parakeet, pyannote)
  • Development and maintenance of public services
