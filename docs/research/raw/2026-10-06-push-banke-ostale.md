## 3. Takeaways for a notification-listener gateway
- Only confirmed OFFICIAL push-for-priljev in this segment: Partner banka (partner.mobile), Podravska (POBAgo, personal+business), KentBank (KentPro; user review says notification may lack amount).
- The "banksoft" family (Agram, IKB, Slatinska, Samoborska, Karlovacka, Kovanica?) shows no push in descriptions; they sell paid/free SMS instead (Slatinska 0.05 EUR/msg + 1.18 EUR/mo; Agram 0.80 EUR/mo; Podravska POBAsms price unknown).
- SMS alternative for Podravska has a documented example format with amount and balance but no payer or poziv na broj. No official example found anywhere of a push containing payer name or poziv na broj for these banks.
- Next verification step (not done, needs a device or customer accounts): install the apps, trigger a test transfer, and capture the real notification text; also open each bank's Play "Data safety"/"What's new" and FAQ for notification settings.
- Tool limits: several sites returned truncated or blocked content (Revolut help 403, Agram TLS error); snippets via search are SECONDARY.
