## Summary: wrappers vs own processing

- Pure Stripe/PayPal wrappers (own fee on top): Donorbox, Givebutter (tip-funded), Fundraise Up, Donately, WhyDonate (Stripe + optional tip), GoGetFunding, Kickstarter (Stripe), Indiegogo.
- Own/legal-recipient models: Every.org and PayPal Giving Fund (re-grant, MoR); Ulule, JustGiving, betterplace, HelloAsso, RaiseNow (own PSP flows, details unverified).
- Native social = platform is MoR, rev-share 50-97%, app-store cut on iOS (YouTube ~49% effective, Apple podcasts 30%, Telegram ~30%).
- Truly available in Croatia (best evidence): YouTube (official), Twitch (SEPA payout), Facebook Stars, Apple Podcasts, probably Instagram, X, WhyDonate, Ulule (SEPA), Indiegogo.
- Not available / unlikely in HR: Kickstarter, Zeffy, Discord, Every.org, HelloAsso (FR only), Givebutter, GoFundMe (not listed), Spotify subscriptions.
