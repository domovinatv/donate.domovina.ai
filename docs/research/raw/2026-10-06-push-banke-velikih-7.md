## Conclusions
- Only two banks have official wording explicitly promising a push on incoming money: Zagrebačka banka (retail m-zaba) and Addiko (Addiko Mobile). OTPgo probable but unquoted.
- PBZ has configurable account notifications (priljev likely, not stated). Erste George has free "Stanje računa" push (priljev not explicit). RBA and HPB: no evidence of priljev push in their mobile guides.
- No bank page I read documents notification text (payer, reference, balance). Addiko's wording implies a short alert that requires login for detail. A listener needs on-device testing per bank. Treat parsing of payer or poziv na broj as unproven.
- Not found at all: forum.hr / reddit anecdotes with concrete notification text, and SMS prices for most banks.
- Business segment is the weakest area: only ZABA business (card push only) and PBZ business (generic) have official docs.
- Real-time: ZABA claims "odmah"; the rest unverified. Android doze/battery restrictions on the dedicated phone can also delay delivery.
